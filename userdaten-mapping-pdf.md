# User Datafields (FixitHub)

## Übersicht aller Userdaten-Felder (neues System)

- email (String, Pflichtfeld, unique, lowercase)
- password (String, Hash, nicht im Klartext importieren)

- lastName (String)
- surname (String)
- name (String)
- phone (String)
- company (String)
- country (String)
- vatId (String)
- customerNumber (String)
- customerGroup (String)
- primaryCustomerGroupId (ObjectId, Ref: CustomerGroup)
- customerGroupIds (Array of ObjectId, Ref: CustomerGroup)
- salutation (String, Enum: ['', 'Mr', 'Ms', 'Mrs', 'Dr', 'Prof'])
- title (String)
- addressAddition (String)
- customerOrigin (String)
- postId (String)
- newsletter (Boolean)
- comment (String)
- paymentMethod (String)
- paymentTerms (String)
- internalKey (String)
- status (String, Enum: ['active', 'inactive', 'suspended', 'blocked'])
- discount (Number, 0-100)
- role (String, Enum: ['customer', 'staff', 'admin'])
- department (String, staff only)
- specializations (Array of String, staff only)
- addOnCapabilities (Array of String, staff only)
- employmentStartDate (Date, staff only)
- employmentEndDate (Date, staff only)
- skills (Array: {name, level}, staff only)
- invoiceAddress (Objekt: street, city, state, zipCode, country)
- paymentAddress (Objekt: street, city, state, zipCode, country, sameAsInvoice)
- avatar (String, URL)
- preferences (Objekt: notifications, communication, language)
- totalOrders (Number)
- totalSpent (Number)
- createdAt (Date)
- lastLoginAt (Date)
- isActive (Boolean)
- refreshToken (String, UUID)
- passwordResetToken (String)
- passwordResetExpires (Date)
- currentStatus (String, Enum: ['offline', 'online', 'working', 'pending', 'on_break'])
- currentSessionId (String, UUID)
- lastClockIn (Date)
- lastClockOut (Date)
- currentOrderId (ObjectId, Ref: Order)
- currentOrderNumber (String)
- lastActivity (Date)
- totalHoursWorked (Number)
- hoursThisWeek (Number)
- hoursThisMonth (Number)
